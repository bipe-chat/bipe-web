# Privacy policy
_Bipe, test version. Updated 13 October 2026._

Bipe is a Brazilian messaging app, still in testing. This page tells you plainly what data we keep, where it lives and why. The Portuguese version is the reference text.

## Who looks after your data
During testing, Bipe is run by Envisioning. Questions or requests about your data: [Contact us](/en/partners/#form).

## What we keep
- **Your account:** username, display name and a protected form of your password. We never see the password itself.
- **Your email:** used so you can sign in and get back into your account. We don't send marketing and never show your email to anyone in the app.
- **Waitlist:** if you leave your email on the site to hear when the app comes to your phone, we store the email, the platform (Android or iPhone), the language and the site page where you signed up. We send one email when the app is out, then delete your address. It is used for nothing else.
- **Partner request:** if an organisation uses the form on the partners page, we store the organisation's name, its kind, the name and email of the person who wrote, the message and the language. The team gets a notice with this data by email. It is used only to answer and is deleted after one year, or sooner if you ask.
- **Your messages:** private conversations are end-to-end encrypted. The server stores them scrambled and can't read them. In rooms without encryption, the server stores the text.
- **Connection data:** when you connect, we log the IP address, date and time. Brazil's Marco Civil da Internet (article 15) requires us to keep these logs for 6 months.
- **Photos and files:** what you send stays on the server so the people you send it to can download it.

## What we don't do
- No ads.
- We don't sell or pass your data to advertisers.
- No third-party analytics or crash reporting. On the site we test which home page title works best: your browser keeps which version you saw, and the server only adds up visits and clicks per version, without knowing who visited.
- We don't ask for your phone number.

## Where your data lives
In Brazil. The servers are in São Paulo, with a Brazilian cloud company. Bipe doesn't exchange messages with other servers: federation is off.

## Notifications
Once notifications are on, Apple's notification service will only get an alert that a message arrived, never the content.

## Your rights (LGPD)
You can ask to see, correct or delete your data and your account at the email above. You can also delete your account right in the app.

## How long
As long as your account exists. Connection logs stay 6 months, as the law requires. When testing ends, we may delete test accounts and messages, always with notice first.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

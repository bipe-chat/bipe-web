# Public feed rules
_Updated 13 November 2026._

A public feed shows a group's posts on the web, at bipe.chat/@name. To build that page, Bipe keeps an open copy of the posts. So Bipe is responsible for what stays up, and takes down what this page lists.

## What comes down as soon as Bipe sees it
- Sexual abuse of a child or teenager. Bipe also tells the authorities.
- An intimate image of a person, posted without their consent.
- Scams or fraud, such as a fake link or a request for payment.
- Threats, incitement to violence, or hate against a group of people.
- Someone's personal data exposed for no reason, such as an ID document, an address or a phone number.

## What comes down only with a court order
A post that someone considers an offence to their honour, when it is not on the list above.

## How to report
Tap "Denunciar" under the post and pick the reason. You do not need a Bipe account, and the report does not carry your name. If someone posted an intimate image of you, report it the same way.

## How fast
Bipe reviews each report within 24 hours.

## What the feed owner gets
The owner gets a notice in the app with the post, the rule and what Bipe did. Within 30 days, the owner can ask for a review through support. A person looks at the request and answers within 7 days. If the request is right, the post goes back on the feed.

## Comments
Comments stay in the group, with end-to-end encryption, and Bipe cannot read them. The group's admins moderate the comments.

## Transparency
Bipe publishes how many posts it took down, by reason and by where the request came from: a report, a court order, or Bipe's own decision.
